Context

Gabriel had been corresponding with the Billion Dollar Build / Perplexity Fund team after submitting an application for myRadOne. The thread contained a submission confirmation, a rejection notice, a question about a public project gallery, and a reply pointing to the community Discord.

Like most real founder conversations, the thread was simultaneously personal, logistical, and strategically relevant — not tidy material for a typical search or research workflow.

Objective

Gabriel wanted to know whether Comet could do something genuinely useful with that live, messy browser context: extract what mattered, identify the right people at Perplexity Fund, and surface direct contact paths for a credible follow-up.

The test was not just information retrieval. It was whether a browser-native AI workflow could close the gap between passive reading and active relationship development.

The browser should be an environment for thinking and acting, not just a window for reading.

Gabriel Casalduc — myRadOne.com · DIVERGENT.health

Workflow

1

Page-grounded summarization

Comet read the open Gmail thread in full and extracted the essential facts: submission confirmed, finalist stage not reached, gallery inquiry answered with a Discord link, draft reply in progress.

2

Team identification

Public sources were searched to identify the most clearly verified members of the Perplexity Fund team — the actual people behind the fund, not just an inbox address.

3

Contact resolution

For each person identified, at least one viable outreach path was surfaced from public data: individual inboxes, shared team inboxes, and confidence levels for each.

System map

Three distinct data layers — the active tab, the AI reasoning layer, and the public web — were combined in one unbroken browser-native session.
